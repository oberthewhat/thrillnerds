Park Hours: 10:30 AM – 9:00 PM

No strict schedule — come for whatever parts of the day work for you!

Parking

Important

Parking is a separate fee from your ticket and is paid at the gate.

  • Regular parking — standard lot
  • Preferred parking — closer to the entrance
  • VIP / Covered parking — covered and closest to the entrance

If you want to be there at opening, aim to arrive by 9:45 AM. Getting through parking, security, and the entrance takes time — especially between 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM.

Most of the weekend crowd heads to Aquatica next door, so the SeaWorld side tends to be much less crowded than you'd expect.

Waterpark (Aquatica)

There are no group plans for Aquatica. Everyone is welcome to go, but the main group will be on the SeaWorld side for shows and animals.
